Left: Wuji SDK (Studio hand model) · Right: re-fit skeleton + MANO mesh on the head camera; yellow dots = the five EMF coils as measured (identical on both sides)
open video ↗

Both sides use the same tracker poses and the same (refined) tracker and camera calibration; only the hand model differs. The mesh is MANO, its shape fitted once to each version's own bone lengths, posed per frame from that version's 21 keypoints and stretched segment by segment to that skeleton, so each mesh lies on its own skeleton (where a finger looks oddly proportioned, that is the skeleton's segment split, which is not well determined yet). The glove's fingertip is not measured directly: the coil (on the nail) is, and the model places the fingertip at the coil plus an offset.

The fit is a non-linear least-squares problem over 400 frames: for every frame and finger, the coil pose predicted by the hand model (housing → wrist → palm → joints → fingertip → coil offset) is matched to the measured coil pose; the take-wide numbers above and the per-frame joint angles are solved together.
