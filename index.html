Showing and hiding parts

  • Open the scene tree: the tree icon at the top right of the key frame, or at the bottom right of the video (next to the speed control).
  • Click an eye icon to hide or show that part; click ▸ to open the parts inside it.
  • cameras → head, left_wrist, right_wrist → points (depth) and frustum (camera pose with its image)
  • hands → left, right → mesh, skeleton, emf (glove coils)
  • trackers → the Vive trackers
  • Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. In the video, what you hide stays hidden while it plays.
