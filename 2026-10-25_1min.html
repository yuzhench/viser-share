What is shown

  • Hand model: a MANO hand fitted to the glove's raw coil measurements, not the glove software's own skeleton. Skeleton and mesh are the same hand.
  • Calibration: the refined calibration of this take: the head camera fitted to the tracker model in its own depth, the wrist cameras fitted to the head camera's point cloud, and each wrist tracker's turn on its bracket.
