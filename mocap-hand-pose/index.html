ARCTIC · OakInk2 · HOT3D

Optical-mocap hand pose: ARCTIC / OakInk2 / HOT3D

Three datasets whose hand pose comes from marker-based optical motion capture. Each video is the dataset's first-person camera with the dataset's hand pose projected onto the image as a 21-joint skeleton, both hands.
The clips are taken from the source sequences. The skeleton shows joint positions only; wrist and finger rotations are not drawn. In all three datasets the hands carry mocap markers, so they are not bare hands.

ARCTIC

Green = right hand · blue = left hand · red dots = the 21 joints. Real-time playback at 30 fps, full sequences. Projection chain: MANO forward kinematics → world to first-person camera → lens distortion → intrinsics.
Frames with the wrist outside the image are at the start and end of a sequence, before the hands enter or after they leave the view.

OakInk2

Each clip draws two projection chains: green = camera-space joints through the intrinsics; magenta = world-space joints through the extrinsics, then the intrinsics. The two differ by 0.00 px on all 16 clips, so the colours overlap.
Every 4th frame is shown at 20 fps, about 2.7× real time. The text at the top of each video is the renderer's own header (sequence name and frame counter).

HOT3D

Green = camera-space joints through the intrinsics; magenta = world-space joints through the extrinsics, then the intrinsics; the largest difference between the two is 5.7e-04 px.
Every frame is shown at 20 fps, about 0.67× real time. Aria clips are colour (1408×1408); the Quest 3 tracking cameras are monochrome (1024×1280).
