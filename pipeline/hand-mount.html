Tracker → hand mount calibration

The chain from tracker to wrist

The tracker hangs from the glove through a printed bracket. Every link is fixed by the bracket's CAD or by the glove, except one angle.

The one unknown, and how the keyed mount removes it

Until now the tracker was held only by its 1/4" screw, so it could stop at any angle θ. Next, a pin on the bracket goes into the stabilising-pin recess on the tracker's base: the tracker can then only sit at the bracket's angle, so θ is known.

What it means for the data

With the keyed mount this block needs no calibration at all. Recordings made before it are calibrated afterwards, take by take, to almost no remaining error.
