Raw recording

The wearable rig

Each hand carries a tracker and a wrist camera on one printed bracket, screwed to the glove's transmitter. The head carries a tracker and the head camera. Trackers get their poses from the base stations.

One host clock

Streams run at their own rates and are not triggered together. Every sample carries its host time, so playback interpolates trackers and skeletons to each camera frame's own time.

Where the data goes

Every file is hash-checked on the NAS against the capture laptop's SHA256SUMS. The clip editor's cuts.json says which parts of a take to use.

Numbers
