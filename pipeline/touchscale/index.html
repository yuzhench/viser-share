CV baselines on TouchScale

Four clips from the TouchScale dataset (Orbbec head camera; its depth is registered to the colour image, with the camera’s true intrinsics). The points are the measured depth; each baseline’s hand is drawn in it, placed in the true camera keeping the method’s own pixels and depth. A correctly placed hand lies on the hand’s points; turn the view to the side to see how far in front of or behind them each method puts the hand. 5 frames a second; the player is under the 3D view. Below, the head camera with every baseline’s keypoints, side by side, with its own play / pause.
