Head camera auto calibration

What is registered to what

The head camera sees the two wrist trackers. Their true shape and pose are known independently (tracker model + Lighthouse), so the head camera's mount is corrected until its points lie on them.

Fit and check, per take

One correction per take, because the head rig can change between days. A quarter of the instants is only used to score the result.

Result: the 54 one-minute takes

Each pair of dots is one take. Error = distance of the head camera's points on the wrist trackers to the tracker model, on the held-out instants. Median 4.1 mm → 1.6 mm.

Run

python scripts/fit_head_cam.py ~/TVAP_DATA/mid1min            # every take under a folder
python scripts/fit_head_cam.py ~/TVAP_DATA/mid1min --apply    # write the ok takes into refined_calibration.json
