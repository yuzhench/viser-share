Hand pose auto calibration

Every frame: two ways to the same coil pose

The glove measures each coil against its transmitter. Our MANO hand predicts where the same coil should be. The unknowns are whatever makes the two agree.

What is fitted: global, per pose group, per frame

Per take: the hand's shape and how each coil sits on its nail. Per pose group: where the transmitter housing sits on the hand (12 groups + finger bends fitted better than one constant pose: 5.91° vs 6.27° mean coil-orientation error, held out). Per frame: the 21 joint angles. Priors: a typical hand shape, the wearer's ruler-measured finger lengths (soft, 5 mm), the housing near the glove software's own value.

Two passes, and what they fixed

Pass 1 learns the per-take and per-group values from samples; pass 2 solves every frame with them fixed. Tracking removed the thumb's frame-to-frame jumps; the joint ranges removed fingers bent backwards. The MANO hand is also what the wrist-camera step (block 3) cuts out of the point clouds.
