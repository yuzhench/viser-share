Wrist camera auto calibration

What is registered to what

The head camera, already fitted, is the reference. Each wrist camera's mount is corrected until its depth lies on the surfaces the head camera sees at the same instant.

How the two clouds are matched

No object is recognised: in every round each wrist point simply takes the nearest head point, and the gate shrinks so that wrong pairs drop out. Surfaces facing different ways (table, box sides, walls) and many instants together pin down all six numbers of the correction.

Which instants are fitted and which are tested (one one-minute clip)

The rule of the current fit (scripts/fit_wrist_cam.py), shown on the left wrist camera of 2026-10-27/20-04-16. The held-out instants are never used to fit; all scores on this page come from them.

Result: both wrist cameras of the 49 one-minute takes

Each pair of dots is one wrist camera in one take (49 takes × left and right; one was not fitted). Error = distance of the wrist camera's points to the head camera's surfaces on the held-out instants. Median 14.3 mm → 5.5 mm.
